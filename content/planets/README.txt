IZANAMI — 1800 TINY ASTEROIDS — RING TEST

- 1800 individual Planet HJSON bodies.
- Names zmoon-0001 through zmoon-1800; Z prefix intentionally sorts after izanami.
- Dense, broad ring extending from approximately orbitRadius 1.62 to 6.25.
- Multiple radial bands with varying dark, desaturated cyan/blue/gray palettes to create layered ring structure.
- 36 Rybek asteroids are distributed through the ring and are substantially smaller and dark green.
- All ordinary asteroids are tiny (0.0080–0.0100 radius); Rybek bodies are 0.0042–0.0058.
- drawOrbit is false.
- orbitOffset is used only for angular phase distribution. Mindustry Planet has no HJSON field for vertical displacement above/below the orbital plane; true out-of-plane thickness would require Java/custom rendering.
- Structure is based directly on the supplied working 600-moon ZIP to avoid the previous parser/name failures.
